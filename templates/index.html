The problem

Crop outcomes depend on multiple environmental and farm inputs. Understanding how these recorded factors relate to yield can help make planning more evidence-aware.

02

The objective

Analyze the supplied crop_yield_data.csv, expected to contain five agricultural inputs and a crop_yield target; no substitute observations are generated.

03

The approach

Clean numeric data, explore distributions and correlations, compare three regressors on a held-out split, then use the lowest-RMSE model.
